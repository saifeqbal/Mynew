The Islamic Declaration of Faith

The Shahada

لا إله إلا الله محمد رسول الله
Lā ilāha illallāh, Muhammadun rasūlullāh
"There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah"

Meaning & Significance

The Shahada is the first pillar of Islam and the fundamental declaration of Islamic faith. It consists of two parts:

لا إله إلا الله
First Part:
"There is no god but Allah"
The declaration of Monotheism (Tawhid)
محمد رسول الله
Second Part:
"Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah"
The declaration of Prophethood
